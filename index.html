About

I am a Magnetic R&D Engineer at LinkCom Manufacturing Co., Ltd. in Taiwan, where I design high-frequency transformers and coupled inductors for power electronics. My work covers the full design loop: first-principles calculation, LTspice and ANSYS Maxwell simulation, prototyping, and test, including root-cause analysis when hardware and simulation disagree.

I came to industry from more than seven years of research in magnetism and materials science. I earned my Ph.D. in Physics at National Sun Yat-sen University, finishing in the top 5% of the program, and then spent nearly three years as a Postdoctoral Fellow at National Cheng Kung University, studying quantum magnetic materials under high field, high pressure and low temperature with neutron and synchrotron diffraction.

That materials background shapes how I approach real components: core selection, DC bias, saturation margin and loss all come down to how a magnetic material behaves under the conditions a converter puts it through.
